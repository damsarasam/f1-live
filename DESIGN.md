# Design System: AeroLabs AI

## Overview

### Which world governs which route

This file holds two visual worlds. The route decides which one applies; the app sets `body[data-section]` from the route table and every world-specific rule hangs off that attribute.

| Route | `data-section` | World | Creative North Star |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/` (the AeroLabs AI company page) | `aerolabs` | AeroLabs AI company page | "The Flight Hardware Showcase" |
| `/f1`, `/f1/sessions/*`, `/f1/live`, `/f1/strategy/*` | `f1` (also the default for any unmatched route) | F1 Data Hub | "The Broadcast Graphics Package" |

**The Route Decides The World Rule.** A surface takes exactly one world, chosen by its route. New pages under `/f1` are broadcast pages; new company pages sit outside `/f1` and take the showcase world. Slanted strips, Titillium Web, the carbon panel steps and broadcast red as a UI signal never appear on `/`; Archivo, the pure-black showcase palette and logo cyan as a UI colour never appear under `/f1`.

What the two worlds share, by design: the AeroLabs AI logo mark and its dark colourway, the motion tokens (160ms, the ease-out curve), the gutter and the 1440px column, the reduced-motion handling, and one header element whose nav swaps per section (the company nav and outlined action on `/`, the F1 tabs on `/f1`). F1 Data Hub's own data graphics also cross onto `/` as product output, unchanged in their colour logic: the speed-scale lap replay (`heroReplay`) and team-colour slivers beside driver codes. Each section below opens with the F1 Data Hub rules (scoped to `/f1`) and closes with a **Showcase world** subsection (scoped to `/`).

The Wind Tunnel world that previously governed `/` is retired. Its smoke-flow canvas (`frontend/assets/js/flow.js`) is no longer imported by any page and is not part of either world.

### F1 Data Hub world (/f1 and every /f1/* route)

**Creative North Star: "The Broadcast Graphics Package"**

Every screen reads like the on-air graphics of a race broadcast. Data arrives as timing-tower strips, lower-third title bars and a circuit map, never as dashboard cards. The ground is carbon black, panels sit one cool step above it, and the data itself is white. Broadcast red is a signal colour, not a theme colour: it marks selection, live state and the active tab.

The form language is the slanted strip. Titles, tabs, buttons, segmented controls, the countdown and the weather band are four-point parallelograms cut with clip-path, butted together with 2px seams the way a broadcast tower stacks its rows. Titillium Web carries everything: heavy italic uppercase for titles and three-letter driver codes, upright tabular figures for times, gaps and readouts. Density is high and deliberate, like a TV graphic built to be read in a glance.

Motion serves live data: tower rows slide to new positions, car markers glide between refreshes, the fastest lap draws itself around the circuit, and five start lights stand in for a spinner during slow loads. All of it is cancelled under reduced motion.

The package sits under the Aerolabs AI brand: F1 Data Hub is its F1 section, named in the first nav tab, and the top bar carries the AeroLabs AI logo mark beside the text wordmark. In the Persuade register (the landing page) the package runs at full coverage: a real fastest lap replays across a full-bleed hero as the only image, HUD readouts and a podium tower are read off it like an onboard graphic, and three oversized slanted tabs carry the ways in.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Carbon black ground with three cool panel steps; no light theme.
- Slanted parallelogram strips as the single recurring silhouette.
- Broadcast red used only for selection, live state and leader emphasis.
- Italic heavy uppercase for names and codes; upright tabular numerals for data.
- Rows stacked with 2px seams instead of borders or cards.
- Team colour slivers and lettered tyre discs; colour is never the only code.
- On the landing, the speed-coloured racing line is the hero image and the overlays carry the offer.

### AeroLabs AI company page world (/ only)

**Creative North Star: "The Flight Hardware Showcase"**

The company page is an aerospace-corporate showcase, built in the register of a space-industry company site (EnduroSat was the user's style reference; none of its assets or copy are used). Pure black ground, monumental centred uppercase type, and products presented like flight hardware: a name, a status, large spec figures with small tracked labels, and a real visual beside them. Nothing is decorative. The only imagery is real product output: the latest race's fastest lap replaying under the hero, and the latest classification beside F1 Data Hub; ASTRAEUS, not yet built, gets a plain orbital context diagram.

Archivo carries the world, bold (700) to extra-bold (800) uppercase with tight negative tracking for the headline, product names and closing line; regular weight for copy and the vision statement; 600-weight tracked uppercase for labels and statuses. Structure is hairlines on black: 1px dark-grey rules divide every band and every row; there are no cards, panels or shadows. Density is low and the page is long, centred where it states and two-column where it shows a product.

One accent: the AeroLabs logo cyan. On `/` it is the primary action, the live state, inline action links, focus and selection, and the "AI" in the wordmark. Everything else is white, cool grey and black.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Pure black ground, white type, cool grey secondary text; no tonal panel steps.
- Monumental centred uppercase headline in Archivo 700; product names and the closing line at 800.
- Products as flight hardware: name, status mark, large tabular spec figures over small tracked uppercase labels, and a real visual.
- Logo cyan is the single accent: primary action and live state.
- 1px dark hairlines between bands and rows; square corners; no cards, shadows, gradients or glows.
- Every figure on the page comes from the live F1 Data Hub API.

## Colors

*Scope: Primary through the speed-scale Tertiary and their Named Rules govern `/f1`. The Showcase world subsection governs `/`.*

A near-black, cool-tinted broadcast palette where white carries the data and one red carries the signal.

### Primary
- **Broadcast Red** (broadcast-red): the active nav tab, the pressed segment, the primary button, the strip tag that names a lower third, the section-head sliver, the top bar's 3px rule and the live badge.
- **Hot Signal Red** (broadcast-red-hi): the hover state of red controls, the "AI" in the Aerolabs AI wordmark, the lit start lights, error icons and the top strategy share. It is the brighter red for small marks on dark ground.

### Neutral
- **Carbon Ground** (carbon-ground): the page itself and the inner fill of tyre discs; also the stroke that separates car markers from the track.
- **Panel** (panel): tower rows, chart panels, plan rows, calendar cells, empty and loading states.
- **Raised Panel** (panel-raised): hover rows, the strip meta segment, segmented controls, selects, the countdown and weather band.
- **Top Panel** (panel-top): secondary buttons, the position cell in the tower, the track outline stroke, tooltips.
- **Hairline** (hairline): 1px panel borders, footer and top-bar rules, chart gridlines, secondary button hover.
- **Data White** (data-white): all primary data and headings on dark ground.
- **Muted Text** (muted-text): driver full names, notes, meta copy, inactive tabs.
- **Faint Text** (faint-text): column heads, readout labels, axis text, retired drivers.
- **Strip White / Strip Ink** (strip-white, strip-ink): the inverted lower-third title and the leader's position cell; white plate, carbon type.

### Tertiary: tyre compounds
- **Soft, Medium, Hard, Intermediate, Wet** (tyre-soft, tyre-medium, tyre-hard, tyre-inter, tyre-wet): reserved for tyre discs and stint bars. Always paired with the compound letter (S, M, H, I, W).

### Tertiary: speed scale
- **Slow Blue, Broadcast Red, Top-Speed Yellow** (speed-slow, broadcast-red, speed-top): the three stops of the racing line, interpolated slow to fast along the lap. Used only on lap traces (the session replay and the landing hero) and their speed key, which always prints the minimum and maximum km/h at its ends. Speed-top shares its value with the medium compound but is a separate role.

### Named Rules
**The Signal Red Rule.** Red means selected, live or leading. It never decorates a surface that carries no state.

**The Letter With The Colour Rule.** Team colour appears only as a thin sliver beside a driver code; compound colour only with its letter. Position numbers and codes carry the meaning; colour reinforces it.

**The Line Is The Image Rule.** On the landing hero the speed-coloured racing line is the only picture: no photography, screenshots or illustration behind the overlays.

### Showcase world (/ only)

A black, white and cool-grey palette with logo cyan as its one accent. The tokens are declared on `body[data-section="aerolabs"]` (as `--al-*` custom properties) and exist nowhere else.

#### Primary
- **Logo Cyan** (al-accent): the AeroLabs logo cyan, promoted on `/` to the single UI accent. It fills the primary button ("Open F1 Data Hub" in the hero and the closing band), colours the live status dot, the inline "Explore F1 Data Hub" link, a latest-list title on hover, the focus outline, the text-selection fill and the "AI" in the wordmark.
- **Bright Cyan** (al-accent-hi): the primary button's hover fill.
- **Accent Ink** (al-on-accent): black type on cyan fills, the primary button and selection.

#### Neutral
- **Pure Black** (al-ground): the page, the header (at 92% opacity, sticky) and the browser theme colour on `/`.
- **Flight White** (al-text): headlines, product names, spec and stat figures, the vision statement, row codes and gaps, the header action's text, and outlined buttons on hover.
- **Cool Grey** (al-muted): nav links, the hero sub-line, section sub-lines, product copy, spec and stat labels, captions, under-construction status and its dashed ring, row positions and names, list metadata, footer text, and the resting text of outlined buttons.
- **Hairline Grey** (al-line): every rule (band dividers, classification rows, latest-list rows, footer rule), outlined button and header-action borders, and the boxed tags in list metadata.
- **Scrollbar Grey** (al-scrollbar): the scrollbar thumb on `/`, over a pure-black track.

#### Data graphics shown on `/`
The lap replay keeps F1 Data Hub's speed scale (slow blue, broadcast red, top-speed yellow) and its white car marker with a team-colour centre; its track outline drops to a near-black #15171a so the line sits on black. Classification rows and podium entries carry a 4px team-colour sliver beside the driver code. These are F1 Data Hub output displayed as product proof; they are not accents of this world.

#### Named Rules
**The One Accent Rule.** On `/`, logo cyan is the only colour that is not black, white or grey, and it marks action or live state (plus focus and selection). It never fills a surface, a heading or a decorative shape. In the F1 world logo cyan stays inside the logo mark only.

**The Real Output Rule.** On `/` the only pictures are real product output (the live lap, the live classification) or a plain hairline diagram for a product not yet built. No photography, renders, illustration, gradients or glows.

**The Words With The Mark Rule.** Status is shape and words together: a solid cyan dot with "Live now" in white for a live product; a dashed grey ring with "Under construction" in grey for one not yet built.

## Typography

*Scope: the Titillium Web fonts, Hierarchy and Two Postures Rule govern `/f1`. The Showcase world subsection governs `/`.*

**Display Font:** Titillium Web (with system-ui, sans-serif), self-hosted at 400, 600, 700, 900 and 700 italic.
**Body Font:** Titillium Web, same family.

**Character:** one technical sans in two postures. Italic heavy uppercase reads as the broadcast voice for names; upright tabular figures read as the timing screen for numbers.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (700 italic, uppercase): the session name inside the white lower-third strip only.
- **Hero Display** (700 italic, uppercase, line-height 1): the landing headline inside the hero title strip; Persuade register only.
- **Headline** (700 italic, uppercase): section heads, led by a skewed red sliver.
- **Band Headline** (700 italic, uppercase): landing band heads inside a compact white strip with a red tag.
- **CTA** (700 italic, uppercase): the landing's slanted way-in tabs.
- **Title** (700 italic): driver codes in the tower, stint chart and readouts; Grand Prix names in the calendar.
- **Figure** (900 upright, tabular): countdown, strategy share percentages, tower positions.
- **HUD Figure** (900 upright, tabular): live car speed and replay clock in the hero HUD, units in small muted 600; the driver cell sets its code at 700 italic.
- **Readout** (700 upright, tabular): telemetry and weather values, with units in small muted 600.
- **Body** (400, tabular): prose in states and notes, capped at 68ch.
- **Lede** (400): the landing hero's one-paragraph offer, capped at 36rem; band copy sits at 1.12rem in muted text, capped at 34rem.
- **Control** (700, uppercase, tracked): nav tabs, buttons, segmented controls.
- **Label** (700, uppercase, wide tracking): column heads and readout labels above values. Labels name data; they never sit above a heading.
- **Wordmark** (900 upright, uppercase, tracked): "AeroLabs AI" in the top bar after the logo mark, with "AI" in hot signal red.

### Named Rules
**The Two Postures Rule.** Names lean, numbers stand. Driver codes, titles and Grand Prix names are italic; times, gaps, positions and readouts are upright and tabular.

### Showcase world (/ only)

## Elevation & Depth

*Scope: the shadow vocabulary and the Tonal Stack Rule govern `/f1`. The Showcase world subsection governs `/`.*

- **Strip lift** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 10px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45))`): the title strip, so the lower third reads as overlaid on the page.
- **Tooltip** (`box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.45)`): the chart crosshair tooltip.
- **Hero strip lift** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 12px 28px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.5))`) and **band strip lift** (`filter: drop-shadow(0 8px 20px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.4))`): the same strip lift scaled to the landing's larger and smaller strips.

**The Tonal Stack Rule.** Separate surfaces by panel step and 2px seams. No glass, no blur, no card shadows.

## Motion (/ only)

The showcase page moves the way aerospace-corporate sites do (line masks, scroll reveals, count-ups, clip wipes, light parallax) plus the AeroLabs wind tunnel. Transform, opacity and clip-path only; everything plays once; no scroll hijacking. Content is visible by default: `motion.js` adds `.motion-ready` to `<html>` only when motion will run, and the hidden starting states hang off that class. Print and reduced motion show the final state.

- **Headline line rise:** each `<br>`-separated line of the hero h1 sits in an overflow-hidden mask and rises from `translateY(110%)`, 1000ms on `--ease-out` (cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)), staggered 90ms per line, once on load.
- **Fade-up reveal:** `[data-reveal]` elements start at opacity 0 and 24px down and settle as they enter (IntersectionObserver, threshold 0.15, bottom margin -12%): opacity 700ms, transform 900ms, both on `--ease-out`, 70ms stagger between siblings entering together. Hero items wait 450ms for the headline.
- **Clip wipe:** `[data-reveal="clip"]` product visuals uncover from the top edge downward, `clip-path: inset(0 0 100% 0)` to `inset(0)`, 1000ms on `--ease-out`.
- **Count-up:** stat figures count from 0 to the live value over 1200ms with a quartic ease-out once 40% visible, in tabular figures.
- **Parallax:** the hero lap stage drifts against the scroll at factor 0.06 of its offset from the viewport centre.
- **Wind tunnel:** canvas potential-flow smoke behind the hero and the closing call, full viewport width, top and bottom 14% faded with a mask. Bodies are the headline, the sub-line, each button and the lap's drawn track outline (never a full-width container); each body is an ellipse padded 56px sideways with its height solved so all four corners clear the curve. Options: density 0.7, smokeAlpha 0.38, lineAlpha 0.08, a 600ms start delay so the smoke begins once the headline has risen and the bodies are measured. The smoke runs 10s of visible time, then fades and rests on still streamlines at 0.14. No tunnel below 640px, where full-width content leaves no lane.
- **Lap replay:** the hero replay plays one lap at 4× and rests on the completed trace.
- **Pause:** a text button beside the lap caption controls the replay and both tunnels together (WCAG 2.2.2). Its label always says what a click will do, read from what is actually running: "Pause motion" while anything moves, "Play motion" when paused, "Replay lap" once the lap and smoke have ended on their own.
- **Reduced motion:** no line rise, reveals, wipes, count-ups, parallax or smoke; everything is in place immediately, the tunnels show still streamlines and the lap is drawn complete.

## Do's and Don'ts

*Scope: the first Do and Don't lists govern `/f1`. The Showcase world lists govern `/`.*

### Do:
- **Do** cut every tab, button, segment and title segment as a four-point parallelogram (10px slant on controls, 14px on strips).
- **Do** stack rows with 2px seams on panel instead of borders or separate cards.
- **Do** keep broadcast red for selected, live and leading states.
- **Do** set driver codes and names in italic 700 uppercase, and all times, gaps and positions upright with tabular figures.
- **Do** pair every team colour with a driver code and every compound colour with its letter.
- **Do** use an inset 2px data-white ring for focus on clipped shapes.
- **Do** explain missing data in a dashed state block rather than showing a raw error.
- **Do** give the landing's ways in as 72px slanted CTA tabs (16px slant), with only the primary action in broadcast red.
- **Do** replay hero laps as a ghost lap: the full lap at 32% opacity, live segments at full strength behind the car.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use rounded rectangles; circles are only for tyres, lights, car markers and the live dot.
- **Don't** use glass, blur or gradients on text. Gradients appear only as the speed-scale legend, the loading shimmer and the landing hero's bottom legibility scrim.
- **Don't** build KPI tiles or rounded card grids; data goes into towers, strips and the circuit map.
- **Don't** use the F1 logo, official Formula 1 typefaces or trademarked marks.
- **Don't** let a motion run when reduced motion is requested. Colour and opacity transitions stay; movement goes.

### Showcase world (/) Do:
- **Do** resolve the world from the route: `data-section="aerolabs"` on `/` only, `data-section="f1"` on `/f1` and every `/f1/*` route, with the `--al-*` tokens declared only under the aerolabs scope.
- **Do** keep logo cyan on `/` to the primary action, the live state, inline action links, focus and selection, and the wordmark's "AI".
- **Do** present each product like flight hardware: name, status mark with words, large tabular figures over small tracked uppercase labels, and a real visual with a caption.
- **Do** take every figure on `/` from the live API, showing "--" or a plain unavailable line rather than an invented number.
- **Do** separate bands and rows with 1px hairline-grey rules on pure black.
- **Do** set the hero statement in Archivo 700 uppercase with tight negative tracking, centred, and product names and the closing line at 800.
- **Do** show the completed lap, still, under reduced motion.

### Showcase world (/) Don't:
- **Don't** carry slanted strips, Titillium Web, the carbon panel steps or broadcast red as a UI signal onto `/`, or Archivo, the showcase palette and logo cyan as a UI colour into `/f1`.
- **Don't** use gradients, glass, glows, shadows, rounded corners, cards or panel fills on `/`.
- **Don't** put photography, renders or illustration on `/`; the pictures are real product output or a plain hairline diagram.
- **Don't** use a second accent colour on `/`; team and speed colours appear only inside F1 Data Hub's own data graphics.
- **Don't** invent specs, partners or dates for a product that is not built; label its visual a concept.
